**I am currently alone in line for my first ride of the day at Cedar Point. It’s 6:17 pm, I’ve been in the line for millennium force for 45 minutes. I’m close to the front. Any other ride after this is not important. It’s Cilcan’s favorite ride and it’s so beautiful on this…. Lake? I’m here with my mom and just packed up my whole material Chicago home and am driving to New York with my mom who I only let come on this trip with me if she came to Cedar point.  She is waiting somewhere because she doesn’t want to go on any rides. She was really helpful packing and I know everything is gonna be okay but I’m crying a lot. I cried singing because I’ll miss singing with my friends. I ate a really yummy soaked brioche with honey butter from publican quality bread. My mom keeps saying it’s okay to cry. I think she’s doing a good job because I know thy and she’s just convincing herself that I’m okay after having listened to me. I honestly appreciate that and am very grateful. I was talking to camille yesterday. Crying because obviously us in our home and around Chicago is part of what I will miss most. She said to write about it and I know that’s a fruitful endeavor but I kinda really didn’t want to in the moment. I wanted to let it be uninterrupted, the cry. Thinking about it now I’m still sad. I made Chicago my world and I’m letting go a world I grew so much in. A world I had so much in. A world where i knew people with so much life and curiosity and love. So many people who learned to lead with love. Including myself. Improv solidified that in me. I don’t think it was new for me but it was consciously important. I think Chicago means a lot to me. Because when a place means a lot to you that meaning is inseparable from all of the distinct moving parts that can be meaningful to you regardless of the place. My family means a lot to me in Chicago and they mean a lot to me in La. The ways in which they mean to me are different and I really appreciated my family’s support for my life in Chicago**
