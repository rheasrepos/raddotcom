Recursion’s Clinical Trial
Clearance for solid tumor and
lymphoma drug treatment
Biotechnology in the News: October 17th
Rhea Madhogarhia

The News
FDA has given clearance for the
clinical trial of a new drug aimed
at treating biomarker-enriched
solid tumors and lymphoma
(blood cancer)
Novelty: Recursion used an AI
aided platform for drug discovery,
contributing to an expedited
research -> clinical trial timeline

The Drug Discovery Process
CDK12 (protein kinase)
-

Frequently mutated in ovarian and breast cancer
Hard to drug because CDK12 inhibitors also tend
to inhibit CDK13 resulting in “significant toxicity”

Novel Disease Target: RBM39 (RNA Binding protein
39)
-

Acts as a pre-mRNA splicing factor
Promotes exon inclusion
Overexpressed in many cancers

Identified REC-1245, targets RBM39 and effects
CDK12 without affecting CDK13

RBM39 and REC-1245
REC-1245 is a RBM39 Degrader (a targeted protein degrader)
RBM39 degradation induces splicing defects which downregulate DNA Damage
Response (DDR) networks and cell cycle checkpoints.
Tumor regression

Implications for the biotech field
- New treatment opportunity for
individuals with tumors and
lymphoma who have been
unresponsive to traditional
therapies
- More specific targeting of tumors
with predictive modeling
- Test new drugs faster!
