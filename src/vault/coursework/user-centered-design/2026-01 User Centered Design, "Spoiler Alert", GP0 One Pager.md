Topic Description:

For our project topic, we are choosing to focus on the user population of college students who cook regularly. Oftentimes, busy students who have to juggle both schoolwork and meal prepping forget about perishable items left in their fridge or pantry, causing a high volume of food waste. We aim to design a software product that can act as an “expiration date tracker,” alerting students in the most efficient way when perishable items are about to expire and should be used first.

We have not used generative AI to prepare this assignment.
