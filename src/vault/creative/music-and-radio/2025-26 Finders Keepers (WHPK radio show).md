# Finders Keepers (WHPK radio show)

Radio show on WHPK — interviews and features exploring music across languages and cultures. Originally pitched under the working title **"Cup of Tea"** on the application, before the show had a name.

## What it is
Music + radio + outreach: episodes built around guests sharing songs and stories in their own languages — e.g. World Radio Day, the Indonesian episode (Gati), Czech (Sara), and features with Luisa (abroad), Henry, Asa, Nathan, Miles, and others.

## Where the material lives
Not text — it's media on the Desktop:
- `University of Chicago/Clubs/finderskeepers 2025-26 stuff/` — episode audio (`.wav`, `.m4a`, `.mp3`), video (`.mp4`), and photos.
- `GitHub/finderskeperseclaudeweb.zip` — the Finders Keepers website (`index.html`, `style.css`).
