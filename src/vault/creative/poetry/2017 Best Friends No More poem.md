# Best Friends No More
NOTE FROM RHEA: this was from either 7th or 8th grade during our poetry unit....

Play, talk, chase, eat
Together in one home
Since day one,
happiness.

Playing with her every waking moment
both following one another,
Or even just sitting down with each other
As if brother or sister.

It's been 9 years.
Health, deteriorating
family, worried.
He doesn't understand but can sense
fear
anger
sadness
knowing he's losing her
slowly.

Trying to act as if nothing has happened.
She doesn't play, or talk as much
sitting, sleeping, eating
sedentary as a sloth.
Still together but he feels alone.

One late night, a time of day not many experience,
pitch black, roads clear, only street lights illuminate the path.
Hearing car doors slam, carried in their arms,
he can't hear her breathe.
They rush as fast as possible,
with worried faces,
crying.

White cross he recognizes as
needles,
stethoscopes,
cold tables.
Tears down his family's face,
she is carried to a room

Not awake, he sees her on a table,
sensing her absence,
his family's sorrow.
Teardrops cover each face
she's removed from the room.

Not knowing how to operate,
Without her.
Movements, slower
Family, surrounding
Something's missing.

Play, talk, chase, eat,
Memories of her
growing old,
alone.
