PART 2:

My poem is trying to show Charlie’s thoughts on his intelligence and the way it affects him through time. I tried to pinpoint the most significant parts of his inclination and decline of intelligence throughout the book so the poem is almost like a timeline but it does not describe his other intelligence, but only describes is intellectual intelligence and how it affects him as a whole. At the beginning of my poem when I quoted, “I hope they use me, I rely wanted to lern”, I tried to show that Charlie was very naive but eager for his intelligence to increase (1,4). He thought about why he wanted intelligence and this very line motivated him to do the operation which affected him in a very big way. Another way my poem shows Charlies thoughts on his intelligence and the way it affects him is when I quote, “Upward, moving, like a leaf in an upcurrent of warm air”(282). This shows that Charlie is thinking about how his intelligence is changing and what rate it is changing and then explained in later lines are how this increase in intelligence is affecting him in different ways. The example I gave in the poem was trying to elude that Charlie’s relationship with his dumber self is becoming more and more distant or “strained”. Part of this poem is about Charlie and the pounding going through his head which seems to be his old self hidden inside of him, when he is intelligent, but this slowly goes away when he has permanently changed to his old self. 

I considered using some of the lines of the following passage in my poem, 

“When I touched her shoulder she stiffened and trembled, but I pulled her toward me. Then it happened. It started as a hollow buzzing in my ears… an electric saw… far away. Then the cold: arms and legs prickly, and finger numbing. Suddenly I had the feeling I was being watched. A sharp switch in perception. I saw, from some point in the darkness behind a tree, the two of us lying in each other’s arms. I looked up to see a boy of fifteen or sixteen, crouching nearby. 'Hey!' I shouted. As he stood up, I saw his trousers were open and he was exposed” (100).

I decided not to use any of this passage because even though it has much sensory detail and great explanations, none of the lines would do any good for my poem and if they did, I could easily compress my poem which would get rid of them. The ideas I’m trying to convey in my poem do not fit with most if not all of the passage because they talk about his emotional feelings towards a girl and when taken apart, they seem like meaningless lines. All of this imagery does not contribute any imagery to the feelings of Charlie towards his intelligence.

I titled the poem “I Hear Pounding” because it is one of the lines that I had repeatedly used in my poem for a dramatic effect so the thought of pounding would be in my readers mind. I hear pounding is really trying to show the strain and difficulty that Charlie is going through throughout the different stages of his operation. It puts perspective on how he thinks. My poem is also showing that Charlie’s old self (mentally challenged self) is pounding through his head when he is changing, but when he is back to himself, he is not bothered. 

One image I incorporated into my poem is “a giant sponge soaking in knowledge”(78). This quote not only shows the image of a sponge soaking up water or any liquid but it also is a metaphor for Charlie during his operation. During his operation Charlie was constantly gaining intelligence which relates to the quote because much like a sponge he is soaking up knowledge as if it were water. I decided to include this sense imagery because it shows Charlies progression and how he thinks he is learning and growing more intelligent through time. 

I mainly tried to use repetition and stanza breaks the most throughout my poem to convey a more serious and dramatic tone. I used repetition with the words “I hear pounding” which was also the title of my poem. I wanted the pounding to represent the change that Charlie was going through and the inner Charlie slowly fading, then slowly coming back. I specifically put this on a tab line so the reader would take a small pause to say that sentence with a more dramatic and waiting effect. My stanza breaks were separated with each stage and idea of Charlies thoughts at those moments in his operation. The first stanza was his naive and vulnerable state when he was very mentally challenged. The second was his beginning progression through the operation and on the third stanza I tried to say that he was becoming more intelligent and rising above himself and the others around him. When I use the line &quot;ARTIFICIALLY-INDUCED INTELLIGENCE DETERIORATES AT A RATE OF TIME DIRECTLY PROPORTIONAL TO THE QUANTITY OF THE INCREASE” on its own stanza, I’m trying to show that Charlie has realized something big and the rest of the stanzas show what the quote above means and how it changes him once again.
