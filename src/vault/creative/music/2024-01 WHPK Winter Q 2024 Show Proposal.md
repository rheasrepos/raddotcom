Hello Dean & Alex

I hope that upon reading this proposal you feel inspired to bring back public affairs programming to WHPK. I’ve been wanting to DJ for WHPK for a while now but, if I’m being honest, didn’t feel I had the chops to do so. There are already WHPK DJ’s I know playing the music I like on air, so what would be so unique about me as a broadcaster? While I love listening to music, I also love talking about it, dissecting it. I actively give away aux power because I’m more interested in seeing what other people have to share and say through their song choices. I frequently turn on the radio, rather than use bluetooth, in my car because I love the element of surprise and knowing that someone else thought a song was special enough to put on air.

Basically, I am proposing a show where each week, either I, alone, or me and a friend, choose a song to dissect on air. We talk about what makes the song special to them, how I feel upon listening to it for the first or hundredth time, what makes the song interesting/something worth listening to, and the specific musical choices the artist made to make us feel that way. We’ll discuss rhythm, vocals, instrumentals, lyrics, maybe parts of the song we don’t particularly care for in contrast to parts we really really do, etc. I have a really open mind and ear and would ideally be listening to any and all genres by the time the show concludes (hopefully fourth year spring..…. ). I think any song can be part of someone’s taste no matter the genre if you know what you’re listening for, so that’s what we’ll try to deduce by the end of every hour - intention. Why do *we* listen to this song, why did the artist make that choice, what makes it a part of the genre it’s in, etc.

I have Logic on my laptop or, if it’s allowed, Spotify’s lyric feature can be used to traverse through songs, or I’ll find out another way to rewind etc using what the WHPK DJ station has. My qualifications: I’m really passionate about this idea. I have a lot of musically talented and music loving friends. I am somewhat of a singer/musician and have dabbled in production so I know something about music theory, production, counterparts, rhythm, harmony. Most of all, I love learning, talking to other people, and WHPK! I was also co station manager of my high school web radio, KHWS.

I think I’d call the show “A Cup of Tea” or “Taking a Sip” because I like the idea that we’ll be figuring out what makes a piece of music someone else’s cup of tea, even if it may not be our own.

If you need me to compile a list of students that would be co-hosting each week with me, I can source that/figure that out ahead of time.

Anyways, please let me know if you have thoughts, constructive criticism, a better space for this show to go, etc. I understand if shows can't be added to the schedule or if you’re only looking for DJ’s. Thank you!

\- RHEA
