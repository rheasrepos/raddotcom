The Golden Record

CHARACTER DESCRIPTIONS 

Remus - 23, nepo baby, chronically online, degree in physics, but wanted to major in anthropology. He wants his dad to be proud/approve. He’s okay at his job, but doesn’t love it.

Victoria Vermicelli - 47, jeopardy superchamp, polyglot, she has six sons and is underappreciated (She’s always being like “My kids think I’m so stupid! HAHAHHAHAHHA!”). She’s very unassuming, but sweet. She and her husband are separated. She wants to be appreciated, and she’s falling in love with the guy in the group who only speaks Esperanto because she knows a little bit of Esperanto.

Willie - 12-year-old prodigy who was on Ellen, super emotionally mature, old soul, Remus’ only friend at NASA, makes jokes about “What’s become of this world.” He quietly yearns for a normal childhood.

Mrs. LaDubious - 32, DJ, hyper pop singer, but really wants to write/sing folk music, eccentric, flowy dresses, always making sound bath sounds, always recording people’s weird noises (“I need to get that.”), writes beautiful music.

Olivia - 25, Mrs. LaDubious’ sound engineer, mainly talks to LaDubious, reserved.

Georgo - 38, speaker of Esperanto, parents speak english, only taught him Esperanto
