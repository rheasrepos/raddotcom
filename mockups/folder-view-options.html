Column view — the OG Finder / Miller-column model Click a folder and its contents open in a new column to the right. Click a document and a big preview fills the last column. You always see the full path as columns, so there's no back button and you never lose where you are. Reads like a filing cabinet.
Big-tile grid — the Are.na feel Every item is a large preview tile you can read at a glance: documents show their first page, art shows the art, videos show the still. Click a folder tile and the grid becomes that folder's contents. Most visual and immediately readable; a small back bar handles navigation.
